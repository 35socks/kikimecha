Up and running in three steps.

  1. 1

    Open LiveControl

    It lives in your menu bar as a small LC and starts its own Ableton bridge. No Terminal, no Node.js to install.

  2. 2

    Scan the QR code

    Click the menu bar icon, then scan the code with your phone or tablet camera. Both devices just need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

  3. 3

    Play your set

    Your setlist appears instantly. Tap a song, loop a section, and keep your hands off the laptop.

One-time Ableton setup.

LiveControl talks to Live through the AbletonJS control surface. You only do this once.

1 · Install the Remote Script

Copy the AbletonJS folder into Ableton's Remote Scripts folder. It's bundled inside the app at Contents/Resources/LiveControl/node_modules/ableton-js/midi-script.

cp -r node_modules/ableton-js/midi-script ~/Music/Ableton/User\ Library/Remote\ Scripts/AbletonJS

Windows: copy the same folder to Documents\Ableton\User Library\Remote Scripts\AbletonJS.

2 · Enable it in Live

  1. Open Ableton Live
  2. Go to Settings → Link, Tempo & MIDI
  3. Under Control Surface, choose AbletonJS
  4. Leave Input and Output unassigned, then restart Live

Questions

Does it need internet?

No. Your devices talk to LiveControl directly over your local Wi-Fi. Nothing leaves your network.

Which devices can I use?

Anything with a modern browser: iPhone, iPad, Android, or another computer. There's nothing to install on them.

Where are my notes and colours stored?

On your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/LiveControl.

My phone can't connect.

Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network, and that macOS has allowed LiveControl to use the local network when prompted.

Do I need to install Node.js?

Not for the Mac app. It bundles everything it needs.
