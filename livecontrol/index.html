Control Ableton Live from your phone.

LiveControl turns any phone, tablet or laptop on your Wi-Fi into a setlist remote. Jump to a song, loop a section, mute a track, and keep your hands off the laptop.

Download for Mac See how setup works

Version 2.1.3. Free and open source. Needs macOS 13 or later and Ableton Live. No account, no cloud.

Turn on JavaScript to try the demo.

This is a working demo, not a screenshot. Tap songs, sections and tabs.

Your locators are the setlist.

Name cues in Live and LiveControl builds the list for you. Two small tags change how a cue behaves.

Midnight Drive
Name a cue, get a song. Songs appear in the order they sit on the timeline, and you can reorder them from any device.
Outro +LOOP
Loops that cue until you move on. LiveControl sets Ableton's own loop brackets, so Live stays in charge of the audio.
Afterglow [STOP]
Ends the song and parks the playhead on the next one, ready for Play.

Built for the stage, not the studio.

Everything runs on your own network. There is nothing to install on the devices you use as remotes.

Playing the set

Jump anywhere
Tap a song to jump to it. Expand it to reach its sections, or loop one with a single tap.
Performance mode
A big, readable now-playing view for a second device on stage.
Mute and solo
Control any track from the Tracks tab. Desktop browsers also get keyboard shortcuts.

Making it yours

Colours and notes
Colour-code songs and keep chords, cues and reminders right on the setlist.
Drag to reorder
Rearrange songs with a mouse or your finger. Your order survives reloads.
Connect with a QR code
Click the LC icon in the menu bar, scan the code, and you're connected.

Up and running in three steps.

  1. Open LiveControl

    It lives in your menu bar as a small LC and starts its own Ableton bridge. No Terminal, and no Node.js to install.

  2. Scan the QR code

    Click the menu bar icon, then point your phone or tablet camera at the code. Both devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

  3. Play your set

    Your setlist appears straight away. Tap a song, loop a section, and keep your hands off the laptop.

Set up Ableton once.

LiveControl talks to Live through the AbletonJS control surface. You only do this one time, and your progress is saved in this browser.

0 of 4 done

The script is bundled inside the app. Run this in Terminal to copy it across:

mkdir -p ~/Music/Ableton/User\ Library/Remote\ Scripts
cp -r /Applications/LiveControl.app/Contents/Resources/LiveControl/node_modules/ableton-js/midi-script \
  ~/Music/Ableton/User\ Library/Remote\ Scripts/AbletonJS

This assumes LiveControl is in your Applications folder. If it's somewhere else, change the first path.

Copy the midi-script folder from ableton-js into Live's Remote Scripts folder and rename it AbletonJS. The finished path should be:

Documents\Ableton\User Library\Remote Scripts\AbletonJS

Questions

Does it need internet?

No. Your devices talk to LiveControl directly over your local Wi-Fi. Nothing leaves your network.

Which devices can I use?

Anything with a modern browser: iPhone, iPad, Android, or another computer. There's nothing to install on them.

My phone can't connect.

Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network, and that macOS has allowed LiveControl to use the local network when prompted.

Where are my notes and colours stored?

On your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/LiveControl.

Do I need to install Node.js?

Not for the Mac app. It bundles everything it needs.

What does it cost?

Nothing. LiveControl is free and open source, and the code is on GitHub.
